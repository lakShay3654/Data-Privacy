# Practical 3: Regulation Compliance Assessment of Coursera

## Aim-
To examine the privacy regulations applicable to Coursera and assess how its privacy practices address important legal requirements for personal data protection.

### Organization

Coursera is an online learning platform that offers courses, certificates and degrees with partner universities. It collects account, learning, payment-related and device data while providing these services.

### Applicable Privacy Regulations

#### 1. Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023 –
applies to processing of personal data of individuals in India, including by foreign companies offering services to them. Main obligations are enforceable from May 2027.
#### 2. General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) –
relevant to personal data of individuals in the EEA and UK.
#### 3. California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) –
relevant where its applicability requirements are met.

Coursera's Privacy Notice has GDPR legal bases and a Data Privacy Framework notice, and a separate U.S. State Privacy Laws Notice covers CCPA rights. It does not mention the DPDP Act.

### Compliance Assessment
| Requirement            | Coursera Practice                                                                  | Status |
| ---------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------ |
| Data Collection        | Describes categories collected in detail                                           | safe   |
| Purpose of Processing  | States each purpose with its legal basis                                           | safe   |
| Consent                | Uses consent for marketing, ads and biometric data; has a Cookies Preference Center | safe   |
| Data Security          | States industry standard measures, with no guarantee                               | safe   |
| Data Retention         | "No longer than necessary", no fixed period for most data                          | risky  |
| User Rights            | Access, correction, deletion, portability, opt-out and appeal                      | safe   |
| Third-party Sharing    | Shared with partners, employers, advertisers and authorities                       | risky  |
| International Transfer | Data stored mainly in the U.S.; safeguards stated for EEA, UK and Swiss data only  | risky  |
| DPDP Act Coverage      | Privacy Notice does not mention the DPDP Act                                       | risky  |

Based on Coursera's Privacy Notice (updated September 3, 2026) and U.S. State Privacy Laws Notice (updated January 1, 2026).
